How It Works

1

Select Your Files

Choose one or more MP3 files you want to clean. Files will be processed in chronological order.

2

Automatic Analysis

Our app uses FFmpeg's silencedetect filter to analyze your audio and find silence boundaries.

3

Silence Removal

The app removes all audio outside the detected boundaries, eliminating unwanted silence.

4

Clean Output

Your cleaned audio is saved as a high-quality WAV file in an output folder next to your originals.
